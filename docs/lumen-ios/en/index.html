Flow

Pick, swipe, done

01

Pick an album

All photos · Favorites · Videos · Screenshots, or any album. Choose one bundle to organize.

02

Browse through

Swipe side to side, ★ what you love. Start organizing from any photo — not just the first.

03

Organize the keepers

Tap ‘Start Organizing’ and the tray (Lumen album) appears. Flick rejects upward.

04

Confirm and finish

Review keep/delete counts in the summary; deletion happens once, safely. Next time, ‘Resume Organizing’ takes you right back.

Safety

Your photos survive your mistakes

🛟

Safe to stop anytime

Keeps are saved to the Lumen album the moment you tap — they survive the app closing. Next launch, the ‘Resume Organizing’ card returns you to the exact spot.

🛡️

Deletion always confirms

Up-swiped photos are only candidates. Slips undo instantly with ↩, and actual deletion happens once at the end, behind the system confirmation — nothing disappears before that.

⭐

Plain Apple ecosystem

Favorites and albums are Apple Photos' own. Delete Lumen and everything you organized stays right there in Photos.

Get started

Build it onto your phone

Not on the App Store yet — a free Apple ID is enough to install it on your own iPhone.

Install with Xcode

  1. git clone, then cd ios && xcodegen generate
  2. Open LumenIOS.xcodeproj in Xcode
  3. Pick your Apple ID team under Signing & Capabilities
  4. Plug in your iPhone → ⌘R → trust the developer in Settings on first run
